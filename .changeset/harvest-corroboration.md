Record the first multi-project harvest, including its negative results.

The rotation pass is a harvest first and a tidying second, and its evidence base is every project the maintainer works, not the collection's own repository. Until now no pass had actually swept that corpus. This one searched 614 files across 28 project directories for the origin failure of each `RECURRENCE-THIN` card.

**One find, and it does not move a count.** `downstream-instruction-framing` refuses an ALWAYS-framed decision-rights header in a handoff. The sweep found that framing institutionalized in a second, unrelated project: four phase-handoff documents each carrying a `## DECISIONS ALREADY MADE (do not re-litigate)` section, plus a planning boilerplate that templates it, so every handoff generated from it inherited the framing.

Those documents are dated 2026-04-29 (three of them from one drafting pass), 2026-05-02, and 2026-06-02 for the boilerplate. **All predate the card's 2026-06-07 origin.** Recurrence means the failure happened again after the discipline existed, which is the question `Occasions counted` exists to answer, so none of them is counted. The card stays `RECURRENCE-THIN` at one occasion.

They are recorded anyway, because they are evidence of a different kind. The origin incident was not a one-off misstep. It was the moment a standing practice got caught, and a boilerplate had been emitting the refused framing into every handoff for five weeks before anyone named the rule.

**An open policy question is surfaced rather than decided.** Neither `ADMISSION.md` nor `AGENTS.md` says whether pre-origin corroboration belongs in `Occasions counted`. Under the reading applied here it does not, and the label stands. Under the other reading the count moves to two and the label drops. The maintainer owns which reading the collection uses; the evidence is preserved either way.

**The negative results are part of the deliverable.** A card nobody searched and a card searched with no finds are not the same claim, and only the second one licenses a diagnosis. `closure-mode-at-boundaries`: no occurrence outside the card's own files. `github-pages-deploy-verification`: zero matches in the corpus. `git-pull-rebase-trap`: 28 matches, every one the card, its guard, its fixtures, research about it, or a project convention citing it, which is adoption rather than occurrence. Those last two now rest on a corpus-wide search rather than on nothing having changed recently, which is what the insurance diagnosis needed. `im-up`: the only hit is a skipped-close already counted on `im-down`, and counting it twice would be the fan-out criterion 2 refuses.
