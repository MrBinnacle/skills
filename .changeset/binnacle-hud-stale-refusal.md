The Binnacle HUD shows why a Send was refused only beside the lines that Send carried. Before this, the reason stayed hidden while the lines matched what Claude had, then came back with the old reason when a new line was added. A refusal stored by 4.1.0 has no lines with it, so the HUD no longer draws it; the status line still says "Not sent." or "Changed since Send".
