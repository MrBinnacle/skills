`AGENTS.md` now states when a published card that still counts one occasion leaves the collection (#551). The clock starts when the rule merges and runs one 90-day window. The first rotation pass after day 90 that finds no second occasion and no confirmed outside report withdraws the card on the policy, and the card goes on watch. A guarded card is off the clock. The README states the rule in place of the sentence that called it a gap. No card changes, and installed users need do nothing.
