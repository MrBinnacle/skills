`ADMISSION.md` gains a short section, "Form constraints are not admission questions". It says a card that passes the four admission questions must still take the published form: frontmatter, size bounds, the evidence rows and harness neutrality. Those form rules live in `AGENTS.md` and are checked by validators. The section states that adding a form rule does not bump the policy version, because the four questions are unchanged. No card is admitted or removed by this change.
