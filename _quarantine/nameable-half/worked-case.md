## References

- [A hallucination detection and mitigation framework for faithful text summarization using LLMs](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-31075-1) — *Scientific Reports*, 2025. Q-S-E methodology for quantitative detection of unfaithful summary content.
- [Faithful or Extractive? On Mitigating the Faithfulness-Abstractiveness Trade-off in Abstractive Summarization](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.13684) — the trade-off that makes a more readable summary a less faithful one.
- [FaithLens: Detecting and Explaining Faithfulness Hallucination](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.20182) — intrinsic versus extrinsic hallucination, and why contradicting supplied material is the harder class to spot.
- [Hallucinate at the Last in Long Response Generation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.15291) — faithfulness declines toward the end of a generated summary, which is where a residue or open-items paragraph usually sits.
