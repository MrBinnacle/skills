# The record

Every first-person line quoted on a public surface in this repository is written here, exactly as
it was typed, with the date it was said. A line that is not in this file does not go on a public
surface as the principal's voice.

## Why this file exists

The front page used to open with a sentence the principal did not write. It was generated copy in
his voice, it sat there long enough to look like provenance, and `BRAND.md` then read it off the
page and promoted it to the establishing example of how he writes. Nothing in that loop was
checkable, because the only source was the page itself.

So the rule is: a voice specimen is a line the principal wrote or ratified, cited to this record.
Provenance is a property of the line, not of the surface it appears on, and an unmarked surface
cannot supply it.

## How to read it

- **Verbatim means verbatim.** Typos, double spaces, missing apostrophes and trailing hedges are
  kept on purpose. The roughness is the provenance — it is the first thing an editing pass would
  remove, and the last thing a generated sentence would contain.
- **Do not smooth a line to make it fit.** If the quote does not fit your sentence, change your
  sentence.
- **The date is the date the line was said**, not the date it was published here.
- **Scope is lines about this work.** Personal remarks made in the same conversations are not
  recorded.
- Nothing here governs a build. This is a source, not a policy.
